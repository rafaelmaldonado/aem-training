Eleven weeks · 55 sessions · English

AEM Sites training.

A practical path from AEM fundamentals to independently maintaining, testing and troubleshooting traditional AEM Sites solutions.

  • August 17–October 30, 2026
  • 30–60-minute live sessions
  • Optional weekly practice
  • No final grade
Course objective: enable frontend-oriented developers to maintain, extend, test, prepare for delivery and troubleshoot traditional AEM Sites solutions on AEM as a Cloud Service.

Schedule: Monday through Friday, August 17–October 30, 2026. Monday–Thursday sessions introduce and apply the weekly topics; every Friday is reserved for recap and practice demos.

Learning path

Follow the route from top to bottom. Each stage builds on the concepts introduced earlier; weekly starting examples let participants follow along without completing previous optional practices.

Learning outcomes

  • Explain how content and code travel from Author to the browser.
  • Read an AEM project structure and locate a change in the correct module.
  • Modify authorable components with HTL, dialogs, client libraries and Sling Models.
  • Trace and maintain reusable content across Experience Fragments, Content Fragments and MSM live relationships.
  • Explain query plans and apply bounded queries, service-user mappings and least-privilege permissions.
  • Build, test and review changes compatible with AEM as a Cloud Service.
  • Distinguish publication, Dispatcher and CDN behavior, and interpret supplied pipeline and runtime evidence.
  • Handle integration failures and repeated asynchronous execution without duplicating the intended business effect.
  • Use appropriate tests and measurements to investigate accessibility, SEO, performance and end-to-end incidents.

Practice model

Weekly practices are optional. There are no mandatory submissions, exams or final grades. A common brief is introduced on Monday; participants who would like feedback can share progress before Friday. Practice descriptions and checks guide exploration and self-assessment rather than grading.

The shared scenario is Weekend Guides. Participants who choose to implement an exercise can clone Adobe's public WKND tutorial repository or use the AEM Project Archetype in their own repository and local AEM as a Cloud Service SDK. Each week includes a supplied starting example so that participation does not depend on completing earlier practices.

Cloud access: Cloud Manager, RDE and CDN topics use public documentation, diagrams, available recordings and clearly labeled simulated cases. Existing authorized evidence may also be used. The course does not rely on live Cloud access, Cloud experiments or participant deployments; local SDK demonstrations are identified as local.

Session format

Sessions last 30–60 minutes. The core 30 minutes cover a short recap, one main concept and a worked example. The remaining calendar time can extend the demonstration, explore a failure or answer questions. Optional exercises provide further implementation time outside the session.

Friday recap and demos

Every Friday revisits the week's concepts and demonstrates the weekly example. Participants may volunteer to show their work or ask for feedback. If nobody presents, the instructor demonstrates the prepared example, including a useful failure and correction. Fridays introduce no new topics and require no submission or technical defense. The final Friday follows the same approach, with an integrated demo and retrospective.

Week 1 · Mental model and environment · August 17–21

  1. Mon, Aug 17 — AEM Sites from the ground up: AEMaaCS, Author, Publish, Dispatcher/CDN, content and code.
  2. Tue, Aug 18 — Local SDK, Maven and repository modules: core, ui.apps, ui.content, ui.config, all, dispatcher.
  3. Wed, Aug 19 — JCR, nodes, properties, resources and the separation of /apps, /content and /conf.
  4. Thu, Aug 20 — Sling resolution: URL → resource → resource type → script.
  5. Fri, Aug 21 — Weekly practice review: baseline and first technical diagnosis.

Week 2 · Maintainable authoring · August 24–28

  1. Mon, Aug 24 — Pages, editable templates, template types and initial structure.
  2. Tue, Aug 25 — Policies, allowed components, the Style System and author responsibilities.
  3. Wed, Aug 26 — Components, Granite UI dialogs and property persistence.
  4. Thu, Aug 27 — Assets, references, the Image Component, responsive images and alternative text.
  5. Fri, Aug 28 — Weekly practice review: authoring and content modeling.

Week 3 · Frontend development in AEM · August 31–September 4

  1. Mon, Aug 31 — HTL: expressions, attributes, conditions, lists, templates and context-aware escaping.
  2. Tue, Sep 1 — Client Libraries: categories, dependencies, embed, loading and debugging.
  3. Wed, Sep 2 — Core Components, the proxy pattern, sling:resourceSuperType and the Adobe Client Data Layer contract.
  4. Thu, Sep 3 — Responsive layout in AEM: Layout Container, Layout Mode, breakpoints, responsive-grid output and browser delivery.
  5. Fri, Sep 4 — Weekly practice review: vertical frontend integration.

Week 4 · Essential backend for frontend developers · September 7–11

  1. Mon, Sep 7 — Java, OSGi and Resource API foundations in AEM.
  2. Tue, Sep 8 — Sling Models: adaptables, injection, optionality, delegation, getters and Model Exporter.
  3. Wed, Sep 9 — OSGi services, environment-specific configuration and servlet boundaries.
  4. Thu, Sep 10 — Testing Sling Models and services with JUnit 5 and AEM Mocks.
  5. Fri, Sep 11 — Weekly practice review: quick recap and team presentation.

Week 5 · Content reuse and maintenance · September 14–18

  1. Mon, Sep 14 — MSM foundations: language masters, blueprints, live copies, inheritance, rollout and localization boundaries.
  2. Tue, Sep 15 — Cloud-compatible structure: mutable content, immutable code, packages and filter modes.
  3. Wed, Sep 16 — Runtime diagnostics and asynchronous processing: logs, bundles, components, configurations, workflows, launchers and jobs.
  4. Thu, Sep 17 — Experience Fragments and Content Fragments for traditional Sites: models, variations, references, rendering and publication dependencies; GraphQL excluded.
  5. Fri, Sep 18 — Weekly practice review: content reuse and maintenance simulation.

Week 6 · Repository, queries and security · September 21–25

  1. Mon, Sep 21 — Tags and taxonomy; QueryBuilder and JCR-SQL2: predicates, paths, node types, properties, full-text queries, limits, pagination and result handling.
  2. Tue, Sep 22 — Oak indexes and query performance: traversal, Explain Query, selectivity, ordering, results read and Cloud-compatible index definitions.
  3. Wed, Sep 23 — Identities, groups, ACLs and Repo Init: effective permissions, least privilege and repository initialization as code.
  4. Thu, Sep 24 — Service Users and ResourceResolverFactory: subservice mappings, resolver ownership and closure, request versus service identity, and distinguishing mapping, ACL and HTTP security failures.
  5. Fri, Sep 25 — Recap and optional practice demos: a bounded Guide List and a permissions failure, with allowed and excluded results.

Week 7 · Publish, Dispatcher and CDN · September 28–October 2

  1. Mon, Sep 28 — Author → Publish: publishing, unpublishing, dependencies, target verification and Sling Content Distribution; the purpose of Preview and the distinction between deploying code and publishing content.
  2. Tue, Sep 29 — Apache and Dispatcher request processing: vhosts, farms, filters, rewrites, redirects, headers and tracing a 403 or 404.
  3. Wed, Sep 30 — Dispatcher caching: cache rules, invalidation, stat files, TTL, URL parameters and reproducing a cache hit, miss and content update.
  4. Thu, Oct 1 — Browser, CDN and Dispatcher cache boundaries: Cache-Control, Surrogate-Control, cookies, private content and asset/clientlib versioning; diagnosing stale content from prepared evidence.
  5. Fri, Oct 2 — Recap and optional practice demos: “works on Author but not on Publish” or “old content is still being served.”

Week 8 · Developing, deploying and diagnosing in AEM Cloud · October 5–9

  1. Mon, Oct 5 — Cloud Manager: programs, environments, repositories and pipeline types; selecting the delivery path for application, frontend or configuration changes.
  2. Tue, Oct 6 — Pipeline failures and quality gates: build, code quality, security, testing and deployment; the first actionable error, compatibility during updates and recovery planning.
  3. Wed, Oct 7 — Effective Cloud configuration: PIDs, factory configurations, supported run modes, .cfg.json files, environment values and secrets; diagnosing local versus Cloud differences.
  4. Thu, Oct 8 — RDE, AEM Developer Console, Repository Browser and Cloud logs: choosing a tool for a specific hypothesis through a prepared diagnostic case.
  5. Fri, Oct 9 — Recap and optional practice demos: a simulated deployment or configuration failure, with a locally verifiable correction.

Week 9 · Advanced AEM: resilient integrations and content evolution · October 12–16

  1. Mon, Oct 12 — Apply workflows, launchers and Sling Jobs to resilient processing: retries, idempotency, resumable work, batching and loop prevention.
  2. Tue, Oct 13 — External HTTP integrations through OSGi services: timeouts, error handling, credentials, bounded retries and connectivity/egress diagnosis using a local stub.
  3. Wed, Oct 14 — MSM localization scenarios: existing rollout configurations, canceling and restoring inheritance, localized references, Live Copies, Language Copies and translation boundaries.
  4. Thu, Oct 15 — Evolving Content Fragment Models for traditional Sites: content and fragment references, missing fields, model depth, consumer compatibility and publication dependencies.
  5. Fri, Oct 16 — Recap and optional practice demos: an asynchronous task that tolerates failure and retries, and its effect on shared content.

Week 10 · Testing and observable production quality · October 19–23

  1. Mon, Oct 19 — Risk-based testing: negative cases for Models, services and servlets; AEM Mocks boundaries and real repository/HTTP integration checks.
  2. Tue, Oct 20 — UI regression testing: one critical author-to-visitor flow, stable data and selectors, isolation, flaky-test diagnosis and the role of ui.tests in Cloud Manager.
  3. Wed, Oct 21 — Accessibility and SEO: keyboard, focus, semantics, accessible names, zoom, page titles, canonical URLs, indexability and sitemaps; interpreting Lighthouse and Experience Audit results.
  4. Thu, Oct 22 — Performance diagnosis: separate query/backend, cache, image/clientlib and browser costs; establish a comparable baseline and verify one focused improvement.
  5. Fri, Oct 23 — Recap and optional practice demos: a detected regression and a measured quality or performance improvement.

Week 11 · Troubleshooting and integrated case study · October 26–30

  1. Mon, Oct 26 — End-to-end troubleshooting: browser → CDN → Dispatcher → Sling → Model → OSGi → JCR; correlate the request, time and first diverging layer.
  2. Tue, Oct 27 — Incident simulation with incomplete evidence: logs, status codes, bundles, services, configurations, queries and caches; prioritize hypotheses and choose the next check.
  3. Wed, Oct 28 — Integrate and debug the supplied Weekend Guides case; discuss voluntary participant extensions and verify the complete author/visitor flow.
  4. Thu, Oct 29 — Code review, regression testing, recovery and remaining limitations; consolidate the worked example and offer feedback on shared work.
  5. Fri, Oct 30 — Final recap, integrated demo, voluntary participant demos and retrospective; no exam, final grade or mandatory defense.

Scope

Traditional AEM Sites on AEM as a Cloud Service. Edge Delivery Services, Headless, Forms and Commerce are outside the core curriculum. GraphQL is optional further reading outside the 55 scheduled sessions; Content Fragments remain part of the traditional Sites content model.
