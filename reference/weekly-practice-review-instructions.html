Weekly Practice Review Instructions

The architect gives a quick recap, then the team presents the weekly practice. Everyone submits the evidence; two developers present each Friday on a rotating schedule.

  • 30-minute session
  • 2 presenters
  • 6 minutes each
  • Evidence due Thursday
Purpose: demonstrate working evidence and technical understanding. This is not a status meeting and presenters should not spend their six minutes installing, building or troubleshooting the environment.

PDF: use your browser’s Print command and choose Save as PDF. The navigation and this instruction are hidden automatically.

Instructions for every developer

Submit the following evidence by Thursday, whether or not you are presenting:

  • PR or compare link from the practice branch to your own main.
  • The reviewed commit SHA and the common training-baseline reference.
  • Maven reactor evidence ending in BUILD SUCCESS.
  • The required local Author result and acceptance evidence for the weekly practice.
  • One short explanation of a technical decision and one remaining risk.
  • Backup screenshots or a short recording in case the local environment is unavailable.

Instructions for the two presenters

The architect selects two developers on rotation. Each presenter has exactly six minutes.

Before sharing your screen

  • Open the PR, required terminal output, AEM page and repository evidence before the session starts.
  • Use the exact commit submitted for review; disclose any uncommitted local change.
  • Hide credentials, tokens, license files, private URLs and unrelated browser tabs.
  • If local Author fails, switch immediately to the submitted evidence packet and identify the failing checkpoint.

Instructions for the audience

  • Do not give an individual status update unless you are one of the two presenters.
  • For each presentation, record one observation supported by evidence.
  • Ask one question about the implementation, verification or remaining risk.
  • Compare approaches without assuming that similar baseline code proves copying.

Week 1 evidence

For the baseline practice, presenters must be ready to show:

  1. their personal origin, training-baseline tag and Week 1 branch or PR;
  2. a Maven reactor ending in BUILD SUCCESS;
  3. WKND visible in local AEM Author;
  4. the responsibility of core, ui.apps, ui.content, ui.config, dispatcher and all;
  5. one exact URL → Resource → sling:resourceType → HTL → response trace.

Review decision

Use the shared proficiency scale: Not demonstrated, With support, Independent or Can guide others. The decision must be based on observable evidence, technical explanation and the response to a small variation—not attendance, confidence or number of commits.
