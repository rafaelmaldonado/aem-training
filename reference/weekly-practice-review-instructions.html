Participant handout · Friday recap

Weekly practice recap and demos

The instructor recaps the week and shows a prepared case. Anyone who wants feedback may share progress before Friday or volunteer for a demo. Practice, submission and presentation are optional.

  • 30–60 minutes
  • Voluntary demos
  • No grade or required defense
Purpose: discuss observable behavior, a useful check and the reasoning behind a correction. If nobody volunteers, the instructor demonstrates the supplied example, including a failure and its fix. Friday introduces no new topic or requirement.

PDF: use your browser’s Print command and choose Save as PDF. The navigation and this instruction are hidden automatically.

If you want feedback

Bring only what helps answer your question. For Weeks 8–11, use the cases on the practices page and show your own local environment; no GitHub access, remote repository or PR is needed.

  • State the practice number, starting example and your specific question or symptom.
  • If you changed code, show the relevant local change, command and result during the demo.
  • Show one expected and one actual result, or one before/after check, when available.
  • Identify which checks ran locally and which conclusions came from supplied Cloud evidence.
  • Keep credentials, tokens, private URLs and raw environment dumps out of shared material.

If you volunteer for a demo

  1. State the goal and the starting state in one sentence.
  2. Show the observable result, then the smallest relevant change or diagnostic trace.
  3. Run one control or regression check and explain what it does and does not prove.
  4. Name the responsible layer, one decision and any remaining uncertainty.

A short recording or screenshots can replace a live local environment. The instructor adjusts demo time to the number of volunteers; nobody is assigned a rotation. If nobody volunteers in Weeks 8–11, the instructor walks through the case already published on the practices page.

Feedback

Describe what the example or participant demonstrated, what remains uncertain and a useful next check. Absence of a practice or demo has no academic consequence and does not prevent anyone from following the next week.
