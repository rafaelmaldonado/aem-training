Class 6 · Week 2 · Day 6 · August 24, 2026

Design the authoring contract before the page.

Separate template type, shared structure, creation-time defaults, design policy and page-owned content by lifecycle.

  • 30 minutes online
  • Repository diagrams
  • Local Author optional
Observable outcome: each developer can classify a Guide Page element as template structure, initial content, policy or page content and justify the choice through ownership and synchronization behavior.

Topic summary

Editable templates define the authoring contract shared by a family of pages. They determine which structural elements remain controlled by the template, where authors may add content, which components and styles are permitted, and what starter content appears when a page is created. They do not replace the page component that renders the request, and they do not own every value authored on every page.

The central design question is ownership over time: who may change an element, and should a later template change update existing pages? That answer determines whether the element belongs in template structure, initial content, a content policy or the page’s own content tree.

  • Page references: cq:template identifies the authoring contract used by the page, while sling:resourceType selects the page component responsible for rendering.
  • Template type: it supplies the approved starting definition when an editable template is created. The relationship is a one-time seed, not continuous inheritance.
  • Structure: structural components stay connected to existing pages. Locked components remain template-controlled; an unlocked structural container may still hold page-owned children.
  • Initial content: starter values are copied into a new page and then become page-owned. Changing initial content affects future pages, not pages that already exist.
  • Policies: policies govern authoring choices and component behavior for a template context; they are shared configuration rather than business content.
  • Placement rule: choose the repository branch by owner and propagation behavior—not by where the element appears visually on the page.

Architect preparation

  • Prepare one editable-template repository snapshot or use the supplied diagrams.
  • Include visible examples of cq:template, sling:resourceType, structure and initial.
  • Prepare a Guide Page wireframe whose elements can be classified by lifecycle.
  • Release the Week 2 practice brief before the session; do not depend on a live template edit.

Learning objectives

  • separate the template reference from the page component resource type;
  • explain how a template type seeds an editable template;
  • distinguish the synchronization behavior of structure and initial content;
  • place Guide Page elements according to owner and lifecycle.
