Class 14 · Week 3 · Day 14 · September 3, 2026

Responsive, accessible and empty states

Verify the Guide Card under width, zoom, keyboard and incomplete-content constraints.

  • 30 minutes online
  • Keyboard-only trace
  • Author and Publish evidence
Observable outcome: each developer can execute a repeatable Guide Card check across narrow width, 200% zoom, keyboard-only operation, long content and separate Author and Publish empty states.

Topic summary

Responsive quality means preserving information and functionality under real constraints. Narrow width, zoom and long localized content are different checks, and the browser result matters more than a collection of screenshots.

Accessibility begins with native semantics and continues through logical keyboard order, visible focus and expected activation. AEM’s author placeholder keeps an empty component discoverable in the editor while Publish remains free of author-only or meaningless markup.

Guide Card constraint check

  1. Load configured and empty Author states, then inspect the equivalent Publish output.
  2. Test narrow width and 200% zoom independently.
  3. Complete the interaction with keyboard only and record focus order and activation.
  4. Repeat with a long localized title and capture the DOM and browser result.

Acceptance: keyboard, 200% zoom, long title and empty Author and visitor states pass with observable evidence.
