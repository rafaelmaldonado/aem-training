Class 41 · Week 9 · October 12, 2026

Make asynchronous work retryable

Explain retryable processing, then apply it in a local Author SDK demo with a launcher, workflow and Sling Job.

  • 30-minute technical session
  • 10 PNG slides · Spanish LLM notes on every slide
  • Guía completa en español
Local instructor demo: copy the Java and OSGi configurations into a local AEM project, then create the model and launcher in Author SDK. The first job attempt deliberately returns FAILED after committing one Guide.

Topic summary

The first part of the session explains how a launcher selects an event, a workflow coordinates steps, and a Sling Job executes retryable work. It then covers at-least-once delivery, stable business identity, explicit job results, durable checkpoints and loop prevention.

The local case applies those ideas: a change to /content/session-41-demo/control starts a workflow, whose Process Step queues a Sling Job for version v1. The consumer updates guide-a, returns FAILED once, then retries: it skips the saved Guide and updates guide-b. A manual replay skips both. Inspect the job log and Guide properties to confirm the effect.

  • Sling Jobs process at least once; the consumer reads persisted state on every delivery.
  • The stable business identity is the demo fixture plus version v1, independent of job ID.
  • Each Guide stores processedVersion and applyCount together.
  • FAILED asks the configured queue to retry; OK completes; CANCEL stops an invalid request.
  • The next attempt resumes from each Guide's persisted checkpoint.
  • The launcher watches /control; the job writes under /guides, outside the trigger path.
